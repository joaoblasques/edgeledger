Why this page is empty

Month 1 is ingestion and the forecast log itself. There is no model yet, so there are no forecasts, so there is nothing to score. This page exists now anyway, empty, because publishing the scoreboard before the scores arrive is the whole point: the metrics below were chosen and committed to in advance, not selected afterwards from whichever ones happened to look good.

A delta of zero, or a negative one, is a real result and will be published as such. If the edge turns out to be nil after fees, that is the finding — and a measurement system that could only ever report good news would not be worth building.

Verifying the record

Every forecast row is hash-chained to the row before it. The daily head hash is published in the repository, so the entire history can be checked end to end by anyone: recompute the chain from the log, compare it to the published head, and any edit or deletion anywhere in the history shows up as a mismatch.

This is what turns a track record from a claim into something checkable. Once the log starts filling, the verification instructions will be published here alongside the numbers.
